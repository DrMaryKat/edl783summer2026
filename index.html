About These Modules

These interactive HTML modules are companion materials for Supervision for School Leaders: Frameworks, Practice, and Equity by Dr. Mary K. Boudreaux, EdD. Each module includes self-check quizzes, matching activities, case studies, reflection prompts, and visual summary infographics with active processing tasks.

Modules are designed to be completed in class during the 4:15–5:30 PM block of each Thursday session. They are not pre-class assignments.

Primary text: Boudreaux, M. K. (2026). Supervision for school leaders: Frameworks, practice, and equity. Draft manuscript, Summer 2026 Edition.
